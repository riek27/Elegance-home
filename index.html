Who We Are Peace Action Network for Development Agency

Mission, Vision & Goal

PANDA South Sudan was founded on 12th April 2026 by a visionary group of intellectuals at Smooth Star Academy Schools. We are a newly established non‑governmental organisation based on economic goals, social values, and cultural virtues—void of politics and non‑profit‑making.

Mission

"To promote and propagate for peace, unity and stability among the citizens of South Sudan through public awareness, civil advocacy and defence of human rights."

Vision

"To have united, protected and cooperative communities in South Sudan."

Goal: To achieve unity, cooperation and development among the citizens in South Sudan.
Motto: "Together, for United Society."

Objectives

General & Specific Objectives

General Objective: To work for the stability, collaboration and development for the local people of South Sudan.

  • ✓ Unite the local communities from all walks of life in South Sudan
  • ✓ Protect the citizens through promotion of human rights and fundamental freedoms
  • ✓ Encourage cooperation among people to live in peace, unity and harmony
  • ✓ Transform lives through services and policy formulation on developmental projects
  • ✓ Mobilise masses for community development programmes
  • ✓ Youth and women empowerment by educating and supporting human rights

📜 Appreciation Message

Appreciation Message on the National Diaspora Policy Review in South Sudan 2026

On behalf of the Peace Action Network for Development Agency (PANDA), and on my own behalf as Executive Director, I wholeheartedly express our sincere appreciation to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation for organizing the three-day National Diaspora Policy Review workshop under the theme:

"Facilitating Diaspora Engagement for Development in South Sudan."

We deeply appreciate the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, IOM presented by Mr. Laak, AU presented by Dr. Profess, Ambassador Thiik and Amb. James Kur — the main organizers — and all invited ministers, professors, government representatives, civil society organizations, youth and student representatives, development partners, and every participant who contributed to this important national dialogue.

I wholeheartedly give a special appreciation to Ambassador James Kur Muorwel for extending the invitation to PANDA and creating space for our young organization to contribute to this significant national process. This invitation represents more than participation — it demonstrates trust, inclusion, partnership, and recognition of the role of young people and civil society in nation-building.

The three-day engagement provided an important platform to discuss how South Sudan can strengthen its relationship with its citizens abroad and harness their knowledge, skills, networks, resources, investment, and experience for national development. As PANDA, we are proud to have been part of this historic conversation and remain committed to promoting peace, unity, cooperation, youth empowerment, and sustainable development in South Sudan.

🌉 "May the National Diaspora Policy become a strong bridge connecting South Sudanese at home and abroad, transforming our collective potential into meaningful development for present and future generations."

Our sincere appreciation to everyone who made this important dialogue possible.
Together, let us build a peaceful, united, and prosperous South Sudan.

AL
Alfred Lual Deng
Executive Director, PANDA South Sudan

🕊️ Field Report
📍 Tonj South & Thiet 🗓️ August 25–26, 2026
August 27, 2026
🕊️ Peace · Education · Sport

Promoting Peace Through Education, Participation and Sport

The Management of Peace Action Network for Development Agency (PANDA South Sudan) is pleased to share an important update on two meaningful peacebuilding activities recently attended by our Field Assessment Coordinator, Mr. Marko Akuei Mariak, on behalf of PANDA South Sudan.

📅 Day 1 · Aug 25
Sacred Heart Parish,
Tonj South
📅 Day 2 · Aug 26
St. Stephen Parish,
Thiet

The training brought together 20 participants from St. Stephen Parish, Thiet, and Sacred Heart Parish, Tonj. Facilitated by the Jesuit community and the Peace Committee of the Diocese of Rumbek, the sessions focused on promoting peaceful, free, fair, inclusive, and credible elections in South Sudan.

👥 Citizens ⛪ Churches 👩 Women 👨 Men 🧑‍🎓 Youth 🧓 Elders 🏛️ Civil Society ♿ Persons with Disabilities 🤝 Stakeholders
⚽ Sport for Peace Initiative — St. Stephen Parish, Thiet

Today, after the training immediately, the sports activities for peace kicked off. The main second activity was a Sport for Peace initiative held at St. Stephen Parish, Thiet, bringing together teams and communities through football and volleyball. The sporting activities created an atmosphere of joy, friendship, teamwork, and unity, demonstrating how sports can bring people together beyond community differences.

The participation of players, supporters, teachers, religious leaders, education officials, and community members showed that peaceful coexistence can be strengthened through simple activities that encourage interaction and mutual respect.

📚 "Education can change minds, Dialogue can heal divisions, Sport can build friendship, and Cooperation can strengthen communities."

Our call is simple: Let us replace hatred with understanding, discrimination with respect, revenge with forgiveness, and division with unity.

Rev. Fr. Ochien
Lead Facilitator
Mr. Abraham Maluel
CDOR Peace Coordinator
Mr. Marko Akuei Mariak
Field Assessment Coordinator, PANDA
