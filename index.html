Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

Peace Action Network for Development Agency (PANDA) is a non‑governmental organization dedicated to promoting peace, unity, and sustainable development in South Sudan. We believe that lasting peace begins with dialogue, education, and the empowerment of young people.

Our vision is a South Sudan where every community lives in harmony, every child has access to education, and every young person is equipped to lead with integrity.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

Key moments that shaped PANDA’s mission for peace and development in South Sudan.

2021

Foundation Established

PANDA was founded by Alfred Lual Deng with a vision to unite communities through dialogue and youth empowerment.

2022

First Peace Dialogue

Hosted our inaugural community peace forum in Juba, bringing together 200+ youth and elders for reconciliation talks.

2023

Youth Training Program Launched

Started leadership and conflict resolution workshops, training over 500 young leaders across three states.

2024

Partnered with SSBC

Formal collaboration with South Sudan Broadcasting Corporation to amplify peace messages nationwide.

2025

10,000+ Lives Touched

Through dialogues, radio programs, and community events, our work reached over ten thousand South Sudanese.

2026

Online Peace Education

Launched virtual training sessions, connecting diaspora and local youth in peacebuilding conversations.
