Who We Are

Our Mission & Vision

Peace Action Network for Development Agency (PANDA) is a non‑governmental organization dedicated to promoting peace, unity, and sustainable development in South Sudan. We believe that lasting peace begins with dialogue, education, and the empowerment of young people.

Our vision is a South Sudan where every community lives in harmony, every child has access to education, and every young person is equipped to lead with integrity.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do at PANDA Foundation.

🕊️

Peace & Unity

We believe lasting peace is built through dialogue, understanding, and reconciliation across all communities.

🤝

Integrity

We operate with transparency, honesty, and accountability in all our actions and partnerships.

🌱

Empowerment

We equip individuals and communities with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to create change.

🤗

Inclusion

We embrace diversity and ensure every voice is heard, respected, and valued in our work.

💡

Innovation

We embrace creative solutions, adaptive learning, and new approaches to peacebuilding and development.

Our Programs

What We Do

Practical programs designed to heal, educate, and empower communities across South Sudan.

🕊️

Peacebuilding

Dialogue forums, conflict resolution training, and reconciliation initiatives for communities in conflict.

🧑‍🎓

Youth Empowerment

Leadership training, mentorship, and skills development for young people to become agents of change.

📚

Education & Training

Workshops and online sessions on peace, integrity, and community development.

📢

Advocacy

Amplifying the voices of the marginalized and advocating for policies that foster peace and justice.
